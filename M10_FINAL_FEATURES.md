# M10 final feature set

This version is intended as the final gameplay feature freeze before deployment. It builds on M9 and adds rival traffic, a coin economy, ten purchasable fictional supercars, three drivers, more detailed vehicle lighting and wheel components, and collision sparks.

- Starter car: Vortex GT (free).
- Other cars: Monarch S (650), Nightfall M (900), Tempest R (1200), Aurora E (1100), Raptor X (1500), Solstice RS (1750), Phantom 720 (1900), Spectre V (2200), Zephyr X (2500) coins.
- Starter driver: Nova (free); Jax (700), Rhea (1200) coins.
- Collect a track coin: +25 coins. Complete level 1–5: +350 / +450 / +550 / +650 / +750 coins.
- Purchases and progression save in localStorage on the current browser/device only.
- No real-world car manufacturer logos, copied brand assets, or database/API credentials are included.
- Database idea is preserved in `docs/DATABASE_PHASE_LATER.md` for the post-deployment phase.
